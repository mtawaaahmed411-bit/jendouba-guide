Nature and Geography

Jendouba is located in the northwest of Tunisia. It’s known as the country’s “green lung” because of its amazing landscapes, thick forests, and the fertile Medjerda Valley. One of the best places to visit there is Ain Draham, a mountain town famous for its cool weather, green trees, and beautiful scenery.
